Behavioral safeguards included
------------------------------
- A new differing purchase/import price is appended to history and does not silently overwrite an already established accounting price; the nomenclature is flagged for review.
- Editing/confirming a nomenclature price applies it to every linked physical unit.
- New equipment created through the normal form is automatically linked to a catalog item if none was selected explicitly.
- New imports resolve/create catalog items and support the unit_price column.
- New acts snapshot name/model/code/SN/quantity/price/amount.
- Return acts reuse the most recent issue snapshot price for the same employee/equipment when available.
- Acts with a pricing snapshot lock the equipment selection during normal edit, preventing the saved document and historical composition from drifting apart.
